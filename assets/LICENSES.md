you add an asset file. For third-party art and audio, use assets whose own page clearly states CC0 (or SIL OFL

## Orbital hardware

The slingshot, launcher, metal wall, kickback and flipper meshes are original to this
repository. Their printed shields, trim and fasteners draw on the
[Space Cadet table](https://github.com/k4zmu2a/SpaceCadetPinball/blob/master/Screenshots/screenshot-1.jpg).

## Cabinet artwork

`web/art/orbital-flight.jpg` and `web/art/lunar-horizon.jpg` are original images made for
this project with OpenAI’s built-in image generator. The playfield brief was a portrait
screen-printed rocket and ringed planet in teal, rust and ivory. The cabinet surround brief
was a panoramic lunar landscape with an orange planet, launch station and engraved contours.
Both images were resized and encoded as JPEGs for the browser.

DSEG and Orbitron have a Reserved Font Name. A changed version of either font (subset, converted
to another format, edited) must not keep that name, so ship these files as they are.

`Doto-variable.woff2` and `Orbitron-variable.ttf` are variable fonts: one file covers every weight
(Doto 100 to 900 plus a roundness axis, Orbitron 400 to 900).
